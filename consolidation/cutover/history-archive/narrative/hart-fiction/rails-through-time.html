HART Railroad: Operational Narrative and Schedule

---

Background & Rationale

HART Railroad is a privately held regional railroad operated by HART Industries, headquartered in McKees Rocks, Pennsylvania. It leases the former B&O Wheeling Division right-of-way from CSX (formerly B&O) and maintains operations from Neville Island to Washington, PA, with trackage rights into Wheeling, WV. HART Railroad was formed during the decline of the B&O Wheeling Division in the late 1960s to preserve vital local rail service, heritage passenger experiences, and to maintain industrial lifelines in the Ohio River Valley.

Founding Vision

The railroad's founding vision was the result of a public-private initiative to preserve essential rail corridors in the face of industrial contraction and Class I railroad consolidation. A coalition of community leaders, rail preservation advocates, and local business owners partnered to form HART Industries in 1970. Recognizing the strategic importance of Pittsburgh’s riverfront access and the towns served along the B&O Wheeling line, HART Industries committed to creating a regional shortline capable of supporting both industrial freight and historic passenger services.

In 1976, HART Industries received a federal transportation development grant under the Rail Revitalization and Regulatory Reform Act (4R Act), which enabled investment in infrastructure, heritage restoration, and river-port integration. With this funding, HART was able to successfully negotiate a long-term lease agreement with Chessie System for the continuation of rail service on the Neville–Washington corridor.

Historical Lease Agreement with Chessie System (1977)

As the Chessie System era advanced in the mid-1970s, Baltimore & Ohio (B&O), under Chessie System management, began evaluating branch lines for divestment or third-party partnerships. Among these was the former B&O Wheeling Division, particularly the stretch from Neville Island through McKees Rocks to Washington, PA, and its connections toward Wheeling, WV.

At the official celebration honoring B&O’s 150th birthday in February 1977, held at the B&O Railroad Museum, a partnership was formally announced between Chessie System and HART Industries. The arrangement laid the groundwork for a historic preservation-focused lease. On April 1, 1977, HART Industries signed a 50-year lease agreement for the corridor, committing to preserve freight and passenger services and maintain the river interchange at McKees Rocks.

Terms of the 1977 Lease Agreement:
- Effective Date: April 1, 1977
- Lessor: Chessie System (B&O)
- Lessee: HART Industries, operating as HART Railroad
- Scope: 42.7 miles of track from Neville Island to Washington, including spur access to the docks at McKees Rocks and trackage rights into Wheeling, WV
- Assets Included:
  - Yard rights at Scully Yard
  - Maintenance-of-way sheds at Houston and Washington
  - Limited access to B&O’s legacy locomotive pool
  - Shared interchange privileges with B&O, P&LE, and later CSX and Conrail
- Obligations:
  - HART to maintain track to Class I freight standards
  - Preserve a minimum of three weekly freight runs
  - Support continued industrial and potential passenger service
  - B&O retained trackage rights for emergency detour and transfer moves
- Renewal: Lease includes guaranteed rights of renewal following the initial 50-year term

The lease gave HART Railroad legitimacy and operational authority, allowing it to act as a full common carrier. While Chessie System eventually became part of CSX, the agreement allowed HART to continue operations independent of system-wide rationalization. By retaining this lease through a preservation and local service mission, HART Railroad not only kept freight rolling through underserved towns — it also laid the foundation for excursion services, barge float revival, and historical preservation.

---

River Operations Revived

One of HART Railroad's most distinctive features is the revival of barge float operations at McKees Rocks. A historic rail-marine interchange point, the McKees Rocks dock was rebuilt by HART Industries in the mid-1970s to facilitate rail-to-river car float service, reconnecting rail-served industries with the inland waterway system.

---

Rail Operations Scope

The HART Railroad currently operates with local freight and manifest service between Neville Island and Washington, passenger excursion trains, dedicated interchange and float service at McKees Rocks, and maintenance-of-way operations across the entire corridor.

---

Freight Mission

The core of HART Railroad's mission is to support:
- Local chemical, plastics, and petroleum industries on Neville Island
- Coil and steel fabrication movements from Pittsburgh mills
- Agricultural and quarry traffic from Bridgeville and Houston
- Seasonal coal and coke shipments via the Washington corridor

Freight trains are routed daily and weekly, with staging and crew operations coordinated out of Scully Yard. Empty returns are often reclassified for float service or transferred at interchanges.

---

Passenger Services and Excursions

Passenger service is maintained through two active trainsets:
- Pittsburgh-Wheeling Limited (PW06): A regular shortline passenger consist using streamlined B&O coaches and GP-series diesel power
- Royal Blue Excursion (RBE07): A vintage steam-hauled special occasion train utilizing the restored P-7 #5300 "President Washington"

Both trains operate during peak tourist seasons and support community engagement, photo runs, and railfan charters.

---

Interchanges and Infrastructure

McKees Rocks serves as the key interchange point for:
- Barge float traffic
- CSX and former P&LE freight interchange
- Truck-to-rail transfers
- Rail storage and crew change point

Additional interchanges and yard support are handled at Scully Yard.
