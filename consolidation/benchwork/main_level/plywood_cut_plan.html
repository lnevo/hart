HART main level · cut plan for review

Main level plywood

Every piece of plywood for the main level, laid out on 4 × 8 sheets. Sizes come from the AnyRail plan: the benchwork outlines, the helix bulb, and the two outer main-line tracks for the +4.25 ledge. Each layout was checked in code so every piece fits its sheet with a 1/8 in saw kerf and nothing overlaps.

1/2 in · +4.25 level 5 sheets

Where the pieces go

Please check

  1. The 6 in board (B1, N2) follows the middle of AnyRail's "Helix Top Turn 1" tracks out of the helix, along the neck and through the south opening, and back through the north opening into the north wall deck. It meets the closet deck at the alcove face of each opening. B1 starts square, 1-1/2 in outside the helix edge. Fit that end to the helix exit roadbed.
  2. Closet deck (B2, B3) is now full over the grid: both alcoves and the turnback, split at the middle of the aisle end so the halves mirror each other. The aisle curve is AnyRail's exact outline.
  3. Balloon loop (F3, G1 to G4) is 4 in subroadbed on the AnyRail branchline loop: 28.6 in radius off the west wall, 31 in round the tip, 29 in back to the wall. F3 is a square-cornered block in the SW inside corner (with the 18 in fillet on the aisle side) that carries the loop's first curve, so G1 is a plain rectangle. G4 is a plain rectangle wide enough for the last curve. G2 and G3 are the two arcs round the tip. Widen the loop if your roadbed needs more than 4 in.
  4. North wall overlap. The 0 in track nearest the north wall (about 7-1/2 in off it) runs under the front edge of the 8 in ledge, which leaves about 3-3/8 in from that railhead to the ledge underside. Narrow the ledge to 7 in there if you want the two to clear.
  5. Helix top (H1, H2, L7) uses AnyRail's exact peninsula outline, including the curved south edge. H1 is the west half of the bulb, 48 × 72. The south-alcove ledge's top edge carries straight west to H1, so L7 is one 83 in piece: the south side of the bulb and neck plus the ledge through the south opening. H2 is the rest, the north-east bulb and the neck's north side out to the wall stubs. Add any access hatch before cutting.
  6. SW top (S1 to S4) covers the peninsula and runs west over W3 to the wall. It's split along the backdrop (AnyRail's view block, 1-1/2 in wide, from the wall to 68-3/4 in out). S2 and S4 are 96 in long, from the tip back toward the wall; the wall strip S1 is one 11-5/8 × 66 piece that the backdrop stands on. The SW corner ledge is one piece (L9) with a straight edge on the SW top, so L9, S1, S2 and S4 meet on straight lines. Put risers under every seam.
  7. Longer pieces. W3 runs on through the closet strip as one 96 in piece (W1 grew to 39-1/2 in to make that work), N2 carries the board through the north opening, W1 includes the NW fillet, and the turnback ledge L5 is one 30 × 84 piece. Two joints stay because removing them costs a sheet: the NW corner ledge L2 on its own (merging it with the north ledge needs a sixth 1/2 in sheet) and the balloon loop's joint at the tip (one half circle needs a fifth 3/4 in sheet).
  8. The 0 to 4.25 ramp still isn't drawn: I don't know where it runs. At 2% it's about 18 ft of track.

3/4 in sheets

The deck stops 1/8 in off the walls, like the joists, so two 23-7/8 in rips plus the kerf come out of one 48 in sheet. Every butt joint lands on a joist.
