HART main level · cut plan for review

Main level plywood

Every piece of plywood for the main level, laid out on 4 × 8 sheets. Sizes come from the AnyRail plan: the benchwork outlines, the helix bulb, and the two outer main-line tracks for the +4.25 ledge. Each layout was checked in code so every piece fits its sheet with a 1/8 in saw kerf and nothing overlaps.

3/4 in · 0 in level deck 3 sheets

68.6 sq ft of pieces. The last sheet is about two-thirds spare, enough for ply balloon-loop roadbed on the SW peninsula.

1/2 in · +4.25 level 5 sheets

112.1 sq ft of pieces. The offcuts on the last two sheets are where the 0 to 4.25 ramp pieces come from.

Where the pieces go

Please check

  1. The 6 in board (B1 to B3, N3) follows the middle of AnyRail's "Helix Top Turn 1" tracks: out of the helix and along the neck, through the south opening, around the turnback (29-3/4 in and 24-1/4 in centre-line radii), back through the north opening, and into the north wall deck. Joints are at 5 in inside each opening and halfway up the turnback straight. B1 starts square, 1-1/2 in outside the helix edge. Fit that end to the helix exit roadbed.
  2. North wall overlap. The 0 in track nearest the north wall (about 7-1/2 in off it) runs under the front edge of the 8 in ledge, which leaves about 3-3/8 in from that railhead to the ledge underside. Narrow the ledge to 7 in there if you want the two to clear.
  3. Helix top (H1 to H3) now covers the whole peninsula: bulb plus neck, out to the wall stubs, the same as the 0 in and staging footprint. Add any access hatch before cutting.
  4. SW top (S0 to S4) covers the peninsula and runs west over W3 to the wall, including the corner south of the L9/L10 curve. The seams are at 62 in from the west wall and 48 in from the peninsula's north edge, so put risers under both.
  5. The 0 to 4.25 ramp still isn't drawn: I don't know where it runs. At 2% it's about 18 ft of track.

3/4 in sheets

The deck stops 1/8 in off the walls, like the joists, so two 23-7/8 in rips plus the kerf come out of one 48 in sheet. Every butt joint lands on a joist.
