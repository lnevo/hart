Main level plywood

Every piece of plywood for the main level, laid out on 4 × 8 sheets. Sizes come from the AnyRail plan: the benchwork outlines, the helix bulb, and the two outer main-line tracks for the +4.25 ledge. Each layout was checked in code so every piece fits its sheet with a 1/8 in saw kerf and nothing overlaps.

Where the pieces go

Please check

  1. The 6 in board (B1, N3) follows the middle of AnyRail's "Helix Top Turn 1" tracks out of the helix, along the neck and through the south opening, and back through the north opening into the north wall deck. It meets the closet deck at the alcove face of each opening. B1 starts square, 1-1/2 in outside the helix edge. Fit that end to the helix exit roadbed.
  2. Closet deck (B2, B3) is now full over the grid: both alcoves and the turnback, split at the middle of the aisle end so the halves mirror each other. The aisle curve is AnyRail's exact outline.
  3. Balloon loop (G1 to G4) is 4 in subroadbed on the AnyRail branchline loop: 28.6 in radius off the west wall, 31 in round the tip, 29 in back to the wall. It's only drawn where there's no 3/4 deck under it, so along the west wall the track sits on W2 and W3. Widen it if your roadbed needs more.
  4. North wall overlap. The 0 in track nearest the north wall (about 7-1/2 in off it) runs under the front edge of the 8 in ledge, which leaves about 3-3/8 in from that railhead to the ledge underside. Narrow the ledge to 7 in there if you want the two to clear.
  5. Helix top (H1 to H3) uses AnyRail's exact peninsula outline, including the curved south edge. Like the lower level, H1 is one full 48 × 96 sheet across the north side from the bulb's west edge to the neck. H2 fills the south span and H3 is a 6-3/8 in neck end at the wall stubs. A big piece on the south side would leave a north remainder over 96 in long, which means more pieces. Add any access hatch before cutting.
  6. SW top (S1 to S4) covers the peninsula and runs west over W3 to the wall. It's split along the backdrop (AnyRail's view block, 1-1/2 in wide, from the wall to 68-3/4 in out). S2 and S4 are 96 in long, from the tip back toward the wall; the only other pieces are 11-5/8 in strips at the wall (S1, S3). The SW corner ledge is one piece (L9) with a straight edge on the SW top, so L9, S1 and S2 meet on straight lines. Put risers under every seam.
  7. The 0 to 4.25 ramp still isn't drawn: I don't know where it runs. At 2% it's about 18 ft of track.

3/4 in sheets

The deck stops 1/8 in off the walls, like the joists, so two 23-7/8 in rips plus the kerf come out of one 48 in sheet. Every butt joint lands on a joist.
