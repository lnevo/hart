HART main level · cut plan for review

Main level plywood

Every piece of plywood for the main level, laid out on 4 × 8 sheets. Sizes come from the AnyRail plan: the benchwork outlines, the helix bulb, and the two outer main-line tracks for the +4.25 ledge. Each layout was checked in code so every piece fits its sheet with a 1/8 in saw kerf and nothing overlaps.

3/4 in · 0 in level deck 4 sheets

87.7 sq ft of pieces. Sheets A to C carry nearly all of the deck. Sheet D holds the awkward slivers (neck wedge, SW fillet, turnback rim) and leaves about 90% for ply balloon-loop roadbed.

1/2 in · +4.25 level 5 sheets

97.0 sq ft of pieces. Sheet J has about 60% spare, which is held for the 0 to 4.25 ramp and any re-cuts.

Where the pieces go

Please check the 1/2 in plan

  1. Ledge width and curves. 8 in wide from the wall on the straights. On curves the inside edge is 26-1/2 in radius, which is the inner track's 28-1/2 in less 2 in. Track centres are from AnyRail: 2.8 and 5.3 in off the north wall, 3.5 and 6.0 in off the west wall.
  2. Helix top (D01) is drawn as the full 72 in circle in two halves (D1, D2). Add any access hatch you want before cutting.
  3. SW peninsula top is drawn solid over the whole 84 × 66 with its 33 in round end (S1, S2). The seam is 48 in from the north edge, so put a riser line under it.
  4. Neck crossing (L8) assumes the main line crosses the neck at +4.25 on its way to the helix top. If it doesn't, drop L8.
  5. The 0 to 4.25 ramp isn't drawn because I don't know where it runs yet. At 2% it's about 18 ft of track. Sheet J's spare area covers about 5 ft of 8 in ledge.

3/4 in sheets

The deck stops 1/8 in off the walls, like the joists, so two 23-7/8 in rips plus the kerf come out of one 48 in sheet. Every butt joint lands on a joist.
