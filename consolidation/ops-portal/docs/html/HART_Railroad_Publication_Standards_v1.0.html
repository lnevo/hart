Purpose

These standards define the visual identity and publication conventions for official HART Railroad operating documents.

Organization

HART Railroad

Pittsburgh & Chartiers Valley Division

Neville Island Operations

Issuing Authority: Office of the Superintendent

Publication Sizes

- Employee Timetables: Letter (8.5x11), landscape, tri-fold

- Industry Guides: 5x8

- Train Information Cards: 5x8

- Dispatcher Forms: Letter

- Yardmaster Procedures: Letter

- Bulletins / Notices: Letter

Visual Style

- Warm cream paper background

- Typography-driven layout

- Minimal graphics

- Heavy horizontal rules

- No spreadsheet-style grids unless operationally necessary

- Clean, professional railroad appearance

Dates

Operational documents use their actual issue date.

Historical or thematic documents should use dates appropriate to the railroad's late-1990s / early-2000s era.

Writing Style

- Write as an operating railroad.

- Use directive language (Employees must..., Train crews will...).

- Avoid conversational wording.

Document Philosophy

Every publication should answer the question: 'Would a railroad have actually printed this?' If not, redesign it until it feels authentic.
