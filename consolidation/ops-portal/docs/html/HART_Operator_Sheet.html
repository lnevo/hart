Portland cement plant at Kosmosdale, Kentucky, shipping from the same site since 1905; founded by Samuel Horner Jr. as Kosmos Portland Cement Company.

Supplied cement for early Ohio River lock and dam construction; a fleet of company towboats with names beginning in "K" moved cement and quarry stone.

The company town of Kosmosdale grew around the mill; Horner heirs sold the operation in 1957 and the plant was rebuilt in the early 1970s.

Traffic: cement (HC) and aggregate (HM/GA) in; empty hoppers out. NVL serves the plant.

Pittsburgh Coke & Chemical (PC&C) was making coke and pig iron on the eastern end of Neville Island by 1929. The coke ovens and chemical works that later became Shenango date to about 1930.

PC&C was more than a coke plant: on the island it also made cement, industrial chemicals, and activated carbon. Those other branches became the Kosmos, Aristech, and Calgon operations still switched today.

In 1962 Shenango Furnace purchased PC&C. The coke-and-iron division became Shenango Inc. and kept making coke and coke by-products on the same site for decades after the PC&C name disappeared.

Traffic: HM coke on Shipping 1 and Shipping 2 (no numbered spots). CK1 lifts for South Yard scale. Loads to U.S. Steel Edgar Thomson Works (Braddock, PA) via CSX and LTV Steel Cleveland Works (Cleveland, OH) via POHC. NVL may set out deliveries; no Shenango empty pickup on the NVL return.

South Yard

Classification hub · session start

Session open / class yard. West Lead = SOUTH-SCALE (CK1 weigh at 4 MPH, stretch, no brakes). Exchange: Demmler/CSX off; Island/Shenango on.

TRAINS THIS SESSION

D749 — CSX turn

• Lift West Yard consist → Demmler pickup → South setout to open ops; interchange per YM; west when cleared.

• Return from McKeesport; YM spots West or South; engine to terminal.

NVL — POHC Island local

• Scully → South; interchange per YM; work Island industries + Shenango setouts.

• Finish Island/Shenango deliveries before return homebound. Final South interchange → Scully. Shenango deliver-only on return.

CK1 — Coke transfer

• Calibrate scale. Shenango: set out reloads, lift Shipping 1/2 coke → weigh West Lead → class. Imbalanced → reload orders.

• Same session: reloads South → Shenango when authorized.

BLOCKING PRIORITY

• 1. D749 Demmler → South setout opens the session.

• 2. NVL South exchange before Island work.

• 3. Island/Shenango setouts before NVL return homebound assigns.

• 4. CK1 weigh/resort; reloads to Shenango when ready.

• 5. D749 return → West Yard.


Dispatcher

HART Railroad · session control

Authorize road moves, keep train order, and protect the South Yard block exchange. Crews work from switch lists; yardmaster owns South class tracks.

TERRITORY

• Scully Yard — POHC interchange (NVL start).

• Neville Island / Shenango — NVL local; CK1 coke plant.

• South Yard — class hub, session open, track scale (West Lead).

• Demmler Yard — CSX interchange (D749 start / return).

SESSION SEQUENCE

• D749: Demmler pickup → South setout opens ops; then west as cleared.

• NVL: Scully block → South exchange → Island / Shenango work → return to Scully (Shenango deliver-only on return).

• CK1: Shenango shipping → South scale → class; reloads back when ready.

• D749 return from McKeesport → West Yard.

BLOCKING PRIORITY

• 1. Hold NVL Island work until South has taken the D749 inbound setout and completed the block exchange.

• 2. Island / Shenango deliveries before NVL builds the homebound.

• 3. CK1 scale string clear of West Lead conflicts during weigh (4 MPH).

• 4. One road move at a time across shared leads when crews meet at South.
