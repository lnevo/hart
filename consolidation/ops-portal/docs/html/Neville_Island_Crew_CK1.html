Coke Transfer

Engine:  Unassigned

Shenango Coke Works pickup and delivery, scale calibration, weigh, and sort. Power is assigned by the Yardmaster.

CREW STEPS

1.  Report to the Yardmaster for the assigned engine before going to work.

2.  Calibrate the track scale with the scale test car (see EQ-01 Scale Operating Instructions).

3.  Handle Shenango Coke Works pickup and delivery as directed by the Yardmaster.

4.  Weigh the coke pull on the South Yard west-lead scale at a steady stretch; do not apply brakes under weigh.

5.  Sort and set out weighed cars in South Yard for classification.
