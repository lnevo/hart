# Pittsburgh & Ohio Valley Railway

**Prototype reference for HART** · October 2026

From 1899 to January 31, 1994, the Pittsburgh & Ohio Valley Railway (P&OV) switched the east end of Neville Island: first as American Steel & Wire's plant railroad, last as Shenango's. HART Railroad's Pittsburgh & Chartiers Valley Division is its successor, and this page collects what is known about the P&OV's jobs, track names, moves, and equipment.

Nearly everything here comes from the [@pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad Tumblr blog](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad) kept by Mark Connolly, a P&OV trainman and engineer from 1977 to 1991. Each "p." reference is a page of the blog's PDF export and links to the original post, where the photos are. The crew and their stories are in [Voices of the P&OV](Voices_of_the_POV.md).

---

## P&OV at a glance

The P&OV switched Neville Island for 94 years, from 1899 to 1994. It began as American Steel & Wire's plant railroad and ended as Shenango's. In 1923 it had 2.04 miles of main track and connected with the P&LE and the PC&Y.

## Job board

The P&OV ran five numbered jobs plus an EXTRA. The first digit appears to mark the trick (1 = day, 2 = second, 3 = midnight); that is a reading of the pattern, not something the blog states. The board below is Mark Connolly's answer to a modeler's question about job numbers ([p.2](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/809168349703602176)), filled in with details from photo captions.

## Track and place names

The P&OV's three yards (West, South and East) already share their names with HART's station maps. Its scale also sat just east of S9 at the yard office, which matches HART's scale on the South Yard west lead. Track numbers seem to carry a yard prefix (E = East, S = South, F = furnace; C is unclear). That is inferred from the captions, not stated.

**Crew roles seen in the blog:**

- **Trainmaster:** wrote switching orders and weighed cars ([p.76](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/47021835262), [p.95](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/45908061832))
- **Yard clerk:** walked the yard knocking on cars to tell loads from empties, and weighed cars ([p.31](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/118944091545), [p.80](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/46862990903))
- **Conductor, brakeman and engineer:** the switch crew; the brakeman rode the cut ([p.84](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/46501750755))
- **Car rider:** rode strings of cars down from the dumper, braking by hand ([p.44](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/85923998090))
- **Car knocker:** car inspector and repairer ([p.37](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/96556931550))
- **Track gang / gandydancers:** maintenance of way, based at the shed by S1 ([p.99](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/45830819194), [p.100](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/45830765892))
- **Extra board:** junior men waiting for work ([p.62](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/60848620277), [p.72](https://www.tumblr.com/pittsburghohiovalleyrailroad/47102644216))
