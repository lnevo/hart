**Subject:** Neville Island Operating Session — [date]

Doors at 6:30, trains from 7 to 10. A day session starts with a mid-morning briefing and runs until the work (or lunch) is done. Job assignments are confirmed at the briefing.

Nestled between Pittsburgh and the Chartiers Valley in the Ohio River lies a 5-mile long strip of land. Originally called Montour's Island and later Long Island, it was renamed Neville Island in honor of Revolutionary War General John Neville. It was once farmland renowned as the "Market Basket of Pittsburgh," which all changed at the turn of the century.

The HART Railroad manages the interchange between CSX and POHC and the work of the local industries. CSX local D749 works out of Neville Island, heading east towards Demmler Yard, swapping blocks on the way down to McKeesport and back. The POHC Neville Island turn (NVL) works out of McKees Rocks around noon, heading to the island. Often they wait for D749 to clear the trestle over the Ohio River, and for clearance from the CSX dispatcher. On the island, NVL handles classification in South Yard — receiving and sending at Duff Junction — works the POV interchange with CSX, switches the local industries, and heads back to McKees Rocks.

The industries deal in scrap metal and metal fabrication. Visitors should be wary of the truck traffic — dump trucks and 18 wheelers — and the no trespassing signs. The coke works is constantly sending imbalanced or overloaded hoppers, so we weigh the outgoing coke shipment. The track scale has been serviced and still needs calibrating.

After much local demand, a classic PCC streetcar is back in commuter service, recreating the Coraopolis–Sewickley route.
