Measure before cutting

These come from the drawing, not the room. Every part marked measure first below falls under one of them.

  1. Stick count and length. All the new sticks came as 16 ft. Count them once the bundle is open: the plan uses 4 of 1x3 and 8 of 1x2. Hook a tape on one end of a few: most run 16 ft 1 in, which only adds spare. Check the two 12 ft 1x3s on hand are whole and the offcuts are 42 and 38 in.
  2. North closet box joists, 16. The south box needed 15-1/2, not the drawn 16. Set the north ledger and rail and measure between them at both ends before cutting CN-J1 to CN-J4.
  3. East closet box, 49-1/2 and 11-1/2. The east box fills the gap between the two built boxes. Measure between the north box's front rail and the south box's front rail for CE-L and CE-R, then the depth for the cross pieces.
  4. SW closet strip. Rail 54-3/4, ledgers 14 (south wall) and 35-1/8 (west wall), joists 19-1/4 and 12-1/2 all meet the walls and the peninsula girder. The west wall girder stops at the north peninsula girder, so the strip carries its own west ledger. Field fit them.
  5. Girder flanges and webs. Flanges 154-7/8 (west) and 153-3/4 (north), seat to seat. Measure in place with the seats on before cutting; sticks F, G and 1 have about 4 in spare for a longer cut. Mark the west web's three pieces on its flange where the NW joist and the rim cross, then cut.
  6. Main shelf joists. North 15-7/8 and west 23-1/8 run from the back wall to the web: measure each, the wall is not dead straight. The peninsula joists (66, 59-3/4, 41-3/4) run girder to girder: measure each once the girders are set.
  7. Lookouts and seam blocks. Cut the lookouts to the deck edge once the north girder is up (up to 6-3/4; the 7/8 one can be skipped). Cut the three seam blocks to fit between their joists.

Closet first

Sticks A to E finish the closet: the south box's missing rail and three 15-1/2 joists, then the north box, the east box and the B1 joist. They use only the 1x3 on hand and one end of stick E, so nothing on the main shelf is affected. Leave the rest of E whole: it is the spare for a miscut anywhere on the job. The split 54 in rail and the damaged joists are not in the plan; cut riser blocks from their clean parts and you will have extras.
