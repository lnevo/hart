The helix

A helix is a spiral of track that lets trains change levels in a small space. HART's carries two tracks down to staging in about three and a half turns, with four inches between loops. The roadbed is cut from plywood and joined with half-laps, using router templates made on a 3D printer.

The main level

The main level sits 15 inches above staging, at the top of the helix. Its shelves hang off the L-girder already on the back wall, on 1x2 joists set on mitered 1x3 blocks, with a 1x2 face on a 1x3 flange along the front. Keeping the framing shallow leaves about 12 inches to reach the staging tracks underneath. The deck at 0 is 3/4 in plywood; the ledges and helix top at +4-1/4 are 1/2 in.
