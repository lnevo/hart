The Build

HART fills one room. The track plan went through several versions in June 2025, trying single- and double-track mains around the walls with a helix down to staging, and settled in July 2025 with the coke works on the peninsula. Crews already run sessions on the railroad while construction goes on around them.

The helix

A helix is a spiral of track that lets trains change levels in a small space. HART's carries two tracks down to staging in about three and a half turns, with four inches between loops. The roadbed is cut from plywood and joined with half-laps, using router templates made on a 3D printer.
