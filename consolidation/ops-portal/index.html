Your first night

HART Railroad is an HO scale model railroad that fills one room of the owner's house. It is based on a real place: Neville Island, the industrial island in the Ohio River just below Pittsburgh, and the Pittsburgh & Ohio Valley Railway (P&OV) that switched its plants for nearly a century.

  • When. Evening sessions usually open around 6:30 p.m., with trains from about 7:00 to 10:00. Your invitation has the exact time, the address, and the food plans.
  • What to bring. Just yourself. If anything else is needed, the invitation will say so. Regulars are welcome to bring their own throttle, but there are plenty to go around.
  • What you'll do. Run a train with a TCS or NCE wireless throttle, walking alongside it as it works; throw switches to line your moves, from your throttle or the touch toggles on the control panels; and read the car cards and waybills that say which car goes where.
  • Who helps. Nobody expects you to know any of this yet. There is a short briefing before trains run, you get a crew sheet for your job, and the Yardmaster and Dispatcher are there to teach you as you go.
