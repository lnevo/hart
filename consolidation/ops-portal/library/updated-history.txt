HART Railroad is a privately held regional railroad operated by HART Industries, based in McKees Rocks, Pennsylvania. It leases former B&O right-of-way from CSX and maintains operations from Neville Island to Washington, PA, with trackage rights into Wheeling, WV. Formed in the late 1960s to preserve local rail service and industrial connections as major carriers shifted focus, HART was established to maintain vital freight routes and support heritage passenger operations across the Ohio River Valley.
HART focuses on the late 1960s through 1980, capturing the final years of independent B&O operations, the rise of the Chessie System, and the early formation of CSX. While the railroad maintains a 1970s character, it operates alongside a modernizing industry, building partnerships and interchanges with Conrail, Norfolk & Western, and the emerging CSX network.
HART’s primary customers include Pittsburgh Coke & Chemical (PC&C) on Neville Island, JB Higbee Glassworks at Bridgeville, Universal Rolling Mill near Carnegie, and Duquesne Steel Works via the restored ferry service at McKees Rocks. Freight operations center on moving steel, chemicals, aggregates, and seasonal coal and coke shipments, connecting industries west to Mingo Junction, south to Wheeling, north through Pittsburgh, and east to Bridgeville.
From the west at Mingo Junction, HART receives limestone from Valley Camp Limestone and agricultural products from Ohio shippers. From the north through Pittsburgh, chemical shipments arrive from DuPont Chemical to serve Neville Island industries. From the south at Wheeling, coal from Consol Coal moves northbound for industrial customers across the line. From the east at Bridgeville, industrial components and glassware from Anchor Hocking move onto HART rails through interchange connections.
HART Railroad’s operations blend daily freight service, local industry switching, regional interchange moves, and seasonal passenger trains. The restored ferry service at McKees Rocks extends rail access across the Ohio River Valley. Trains are staged and dispatched from Scully Yard, supporting local turns, mainline freights, ferry transfers, and interchange runs. Passenger service includes the Pittsburgh–Wheeling Limited and Royal Blue Excursion, preserving a direct connection to the region’s rail heritage.
Crew roles include yardmaster, local and mainline engineers, ferry switch crews, and passenger train crews, all operating under a timetable and train order system modeled after real-world railroad practices.

HART Railroad is a dynamic regional carrier based in McKees Rocks, Pennsylvania, preserving the spirit of the B&O while forging vital connections across the Ohio River Valley. Operating former B&O trackage from Neville Island to Washington, PA with trackage rights into Wheeling, HART captures the late 1960s to 1980 era, blending classic freight, restored ferry service, and heritage passenger trains. Serving major customers like Pittsburgh Coke & Chemical, JB Higbee Glassworks, Universal Rolling Mill, and Duquesne Steel Works, HART moves steel, chemicals, limestone, coal, and glass through key interchanges with Conrail, Norfolk & Western, and the emerging CSX system. Whether hauling limestone from Mingo Junction, chemicals from Pittsburgh, coal from Wheeling, or glassware from Bridgeville, HART Railroad delivers a living connection to America's industrial heartbeat, operated under real-world timetable and train order practices that bring history to life.
