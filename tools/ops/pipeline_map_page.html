    Legend and help
    Lanes: the map opens on one card per lane, with its boxes counted by kind and its make panels and check.sh steps, and arrows for the links between lanes (thicker for more; the number is the link count). Click a lane to open it: the other lanes close to one box each that keeps their links, counted. Click an arrow to open the two lanes it joins, or a closed lane's box to open it too. Whole map draws every lane in full. The trail under the toolbar (All lanes ▸ a lane ▸ a file) goes back to any step; so do the browser's Back button and Esc, one level at a time.
    Efficiency overlay (Efficiency, or E): a stamped step, skipped while its inputs are unchanged (make panels and check.sh stamps); a make panels guard, which always runs; on an output, the scripts that write it: red for two or more that the step manifests do not allow (a shared output shows green), grey for none; a step's logged time, amber from 10 s, red from 60 s; a step with no logged time.
    Kind (box colour)__LEGEND__
    Lane stripe = domain. Dashed box = parked, dotted = retired, dashed line = feeds back to an earlier step.
    One file: type or pick it in the File box, find it under Browse ▾ by folder, or double-click its box, to draw its chain: everything that feeds it, back to the sources, and everything it feeds, out to the end products. The second row holds the step counters for each side (0 hides that side, all runs to the end); [ and ] take one step off or add one on both sides. Start ▾ lists common questions, each drawing one chain full width with its main line lit, from where the work starts to what it makes, and the rest of the chain dimmed; a click on a box or the map ends it.
    Find (or /) lists the boxes whose name, path or description holds the text: the name itself first, then names that start with it, names that hold it, paths and descriptions. Arrows move in the list and Enter draws that box's chain; the other boxes fade while the list is open.
    Filters opens the filter panel: lane, kind, status, step type and writers for the boxes, and what the links carry. Tick the values to keep: any of them within a group, all groups together. Each value shows how many boxes have it, and with other filters on, how many of those are left (370 → 41). The boxes the filters do not match fade; Hide the rest leaves them out instead, joining the links straight through hidden kinds. With no status ticked, only live boxes are drawn. Each filter in use is a chip under the toolbar; click one to remove it, or Clear all. List (or L) opens a table of the boxes shown, sorted by any column; a row draws that box's chain. Start over goes back to the lanes with no filters, search or overlay.
    View ▾, boxes and links: Compact boxes (names only; untick for each box's path and description), Group by folder (one dashed box per folder; double-click a folder, or use Open in its panel, to see its files), Edge labels and Highlighted chain only (leave out everything a clicked box does not light). Pointer: Hover highlights and Near pipeline on click. Page: Dark background and Clean view (or H: hides the lane names, step headers, this legend and the overview). Share copies a link to the view as it is, with the selected box, zoom and position. A first visit gets a short tour; ? runs it again. On a phone the menus and buttons are under More ▾, Fit stays out, and the filters open from the bottom.
    Move: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, F fits, Esc clears the selection, then goes up a level. Steps, at the right of the step headers, sets how many steps show across the screen: the lanes keep their full height and you pan sideways for the rest, the boxes growing with the steps, text and all, as far as the lanes have room (up to 2.5 times), and the gap taking the rest; the drawing follows the slider as you drag it (or turn the wheel over it) and is laid out properly when you let go; Fit then shows the first of them from the left, and all of them is the whole map. Click a lane's name on the left to zoom to that lane, and again to go back (a box under the name takes the click first). Hold Shift for a magnifier. Zoomed in, the overview in the corner shows the whole drawing with a red box round what is in view; click or drag in it to move.
    Hover: zoomed out, hover a line to light its chain, what feeds it and what it feeds, and grow its boxes to a readable size; hover a box to do the same for its neighbours. A hovered chain stops at hubs where many pipelines meet (such as tables.xml), runners such as check.sh, caches, hosts and hand work, and does not cross side links that carry nothing on (what a check reads, a doc pointer, a Store pull back into the sources). Boxes more than two steps from the line are outlined fainter. The note by the pointer names where the chain stopped.
    Click: a box lights what feeds it (amber) and what it feeds (blue), its full reach; with Near pipeline on click it stops where a hover does. A line opens the box it leads to; Cmd-click (Ctrl-click off a Mac) opens the box it comes from. A click on empty map, or Esc, clears the selection. A click-chain stops at deploy, host, hand-work and cache boxes: they show, but the chain does not run on through them.
