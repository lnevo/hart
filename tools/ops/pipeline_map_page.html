Legend and helpKind (box colour)__LEGEND__
Lane stripe = domain. Dashed box = parked, dotted = retired, dashed line = feeds back to an earlier step.
One file: type or pick it in the File box, or find it under Browse ▾ by folder (or double-click its box), to draw its whole chain: everything that feeds it, back to the sources, and everything it feeds, out to the end products. A second row under the toolbar names the file and holds the step counters for upstream and downstream (0 hides that side, all runs to the end). Start ▾ opens common chains. Find highlights the boxes that hold a piece of text.
View ▾, boxes and links: Compact boxes (on to start with: names only; untick for each box's path and description), Group by folder (one dashed box per folder; double-click a folder, or use Open in its panel, to see its files), Edge labels and Highlighted chain only (leave out everything a clicked box does not light). Pointer: Hover highlights and Near pipeline on click. Page: Dark background and Clean view (or H: hides the lane names, step headers, this legend and the overview).
Filters ▾ picks the lanes and kinds of box to draw, and whether parked features and retired tools show. Untick a kind, such as scripts, and its boxes are left out with their links joined up; only beside a lane or kind shows just that one (a second click shows them all again). Clear filters, at the foot of Filters or in the second row, puts them back and keeps the file and the selected box. Whole map appears when the view is cut down. Share copies a link to the view as it is, with the selected box, zoom and position. A first visit gets a short tour; ? runs it again. On a phone, Browse, Start, View, Filters and the buttons are under More ▾; Fit stays out.
Move: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, F fits, Esc clears. Steps, at the right of the step headers, sets how many steps show across the screen: the lanes keep their full height and you pan sideways for the rest, the boxes growing with the steps, text and all, as far as the lanes have room (up to 2.5 times), and the gap taking the rest; the drawing follows the slider as you drag it (or turn the wheel over it) and is laid out properly when you let go; Fit then shows the first of them from the left, and all of them is the whole map. Click a lane's name on the left to zoom to that lane, and again to go back (a box under the name takes the click first). Hold Shift for a magnifier. Zoomed in, the overview in the corner shows the whole drawing with a red box round what is in view; click or drag in it to move.
Hover: zoomed out, hover a line to light its chain, what feeds it and what it feeds, and grow its boxes to a readable size; hover a box to do the same for its neighbours. A hovered chain stops at hubs where many pipelines meet (such as tables.xml), runners such as check.sh, caches, hosts and hand work, and does not cross side links that carry nothing on (what a check reads, a doc pointer, a Store pull back into the sources). Boxes more than two steps from the line are outlined fainter. The note by the pointer names where the chain stopped.
Click: a box lights what feeds it (amber) and what it feeds (blue), its full reach; with Near pipeline on click it stops where a hover does. A line opens the box it leads to; Cmd-click (Ctrl-click off a Mac) opens the box it comes from. A click on empty map, or Esc, clears the selection. A click-chain stops at deploy, host, hand-work and cache boxes: they show, but the chain does not run on through them.
