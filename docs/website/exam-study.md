# OPSIG C&E study pages

Rules for the C&E study deck (`www/exam/index.html`), the Extra East game (`www/exam/run.html`) and the `/play` runs. Public facts only. C&E is Conductor & Engineer, one of the OPSIG achievements (OPSIG's study guide and exam, not Certification and Evaluation). The exam has not been taken; the pages are for practice.

## Sources

Seven OPSIG study guide PDFs sit in OPSIG's public Google Drive folder, linked from <https://opsig-ap.org/ce/>. They are not kept in the repo. The Standard Code guide is the first; the others are GCOR, the TT&TO primer, Yard Limits, TWC, Signals and Semaphores.

## Exam focus (owner, 2026-11-16)

- Only the 1.0 Standard Code guide (the 1949 Code, 65 pages) marks rules in red, and its introduction says the red sections "may in fact be on the test". Red set: the definitions, Rule 5, 12(a-d), whistles 14(a)(b)(c)(h)(l), 20, S/D-71 to 73, 82-94, 99, 104, 203, 206, 207, 211, 220-222, train order forms, 251, Fixed Signals 281-292.
- The other six guides count whole. The deck and game stay on the red rules plus those six guides; check any new Standard Code card against the red set first. Non-red items 14(g) and 14(j) were removed.
- Fixed Signals 281-292 are scanned aspect pages (PDF pp. 46-54), read as images. The limited, medium and slow speed definitions are not red, so they have no card. GCOR has no chapter 9. The Signals guide is 5 pages; its p.4 placement diagram is unused.
- The guides disagree on the S-87/S-89 margin and the Rule 93 speed; the deck follows the Standard Code.

## Rule wording

- Every rule statement says only what the cited guide says, in its words. Keep the Code's blanks blank (Rules 85, 93), keep "when necessary" and "should", cite each borrowed fact to its own guide, and keep slang ("highball") out of rule text and options.
- Standard terms the guides happen not to use are fine beside the guide wording ("permissive" for number-plate signals is valid).
- Quote the page cited: the Signals guide says "next two blocks are unoccupied" on its p.5 chart but "this block and the next block" on p.3. "SG · Signal chart" items use the chart words.
- Reason: a reader flagged the highball line (2026-11-17), and a check of all seven PDFs then found 15 more drifts.

## Game rules (owner, 2026-11-22)

The full list for the Chartiers runs is in the archived page `archive/history/wiki/projects/play-up-the-chartiers.md` ("Rules already decided").

- In `/play` and Extra East every question tests a deck rule. Prototype history (the P&OV blog, plant names by year) sets the scene with an inline source link and is never what is asked: "we're not trying to quiz the blog, but use the historical details to build an exam to study the material."
- No disclaimers in play ("for the game", "invented", "the blog does not say"): "we don't have to justify, apologize or disclaim it". The introduction says once where the material comes from.
- There is no daily run; every run is a new draw (removed 2026-11-22).
- Extra East runs 812 east, left to right; westward trains are superior (No 1 West, No 2 East).
- Writing follows the site voice rules: `tools/audit/check_ai_speak.py` and the `voice-reviewer` agent on new text.
