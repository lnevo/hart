# Writing voice

The rules and the table of rejected examples are in the Writing section of [`www/README.md`](../../www/README.md); the `voice-reviewer` agent applies them. This page keeps the owner's rulings behind them and the cases the table does not cover.

## Scope

The filter covers all writing for the owner: site pages, slides, speaker notes, handouts and chat drafts (owner, 2026-11-15, on a clinic deck: "the AI speak we're supposed to avoid"; apply it to everything written). Slogan lines, pull-quote slides, morals, metaphor as explanation, colon reveals ("None of the parts is a model of anything: they're tools"), punchy imperatives ("It suggests; you decide") and "what stayed mine" framing were all flagged. Write what the job was, what AI did, what came out, and the numbers.

`tools/audit/check_ai_speak.py` catches set phrases only. Read the slogan and moral kinds by hand.

## Intros

On 2026-11-11 the owner called the old Control & signals opening "awful": a weighed pro and con ("AI made it cheap to try an approach, which is good, and it also made it easy to build something clever") ending in a moral ("the direction that won was the plainest one"). A first rewrite in a reflective voice was still "quite awful". The accepted form is a plain summary of what the topic covers and the key facts: several signal systems were tried; CATS is used for CTC and PanelPro for everyday use. No narrative, no reflection.

## Cases

- A clause that speaks for the reader or the exam, in the C&E deck on 2026-11-15, was called a good example of AI speak (the rejected sentence is in the `www/README.md` table). Delete the clause.
- Short UI text (tooltips, badges, button labels) gets the fewest words that say what it is. "Generated by script from the railroad's data files, not written by hand" was called AI voice (2026-11-17); the kept text is "Generated from the railroad's data files". A ", not X" contrast tail counts as AI voice.
- The "Worked well / Needed me" labels on the AI topic pages are the series format; keep them.
- When cutting filler, keep any fact or rule inside the sentence.
- Documents the owner submits (2026-11-15, an electrical statement of qualifications): "bulletproof like a dissertation" produced a result the owner called "beautiful but overwhelming". The kept version is high-school level, first person ("I built"), short paragraphs, a contents page and a checklist, with no summary page, figure or table lists, glossary or evidence-label scaffolding. Start at that level even when the request sounds formal.
