Lane stripe = domain. Dashed box = parked, dotted = retired, dashed line = feeds back to an earlier step.
Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, F fits, Esc clears. When zoomed in, the overview in the corner shows the whole drawing with a red box round what is in view; click or drag in it to move. Click a box for what feeds it (amber) and what it feeds (blue). Tick highlighted only to leave out everything else and draw that chain on its own.
One file: type or pick it in the File box, find it under Browse ▾ by folder (or double-click its box) to draw its whole chain: everything that feeds it, back to the sources, and everything it feeds, out to the end products. Boxes off that chain are left out. A chain stops at deploy, host and hand-work boxes: they show, but the chain does not run on through them. A second row under the toolbar names the file and holds the step counters: left is what feeds it, right is what it feeds (0 hides that side, all runs to the end). Start ▾ opens the chains people ask about most. A first visit gets a short tour of the controls; ? runs it again. Find highlights the boxes that hold a piece of text.
View ▾ holds compact (on to start with: boxes as names only; untick it for each box's path and description), folders (the whole map as one dashed box per folder; double-click a folder, or use Open in its panel, to see its files), highlighted only and edge labels. Filters ▾ picks the lanes and the kinds of box to draw, and whether parked and retired boxes show. Untick a kind, such as scripts, and its boxes are left out with their links joined up, so data runs straight to what it ends up in. When a filter is changed, Clear filters in the second row puts them back and keeps the file and the selected box. Whole map appears when the view is cut down and goes back to the full drawing. Share copies a link to the view as it is, with the selected box, zoom and position; the address bar also keeps the view, without the zoom. On a phone, Browse and everything after Find are under Menu ▾.
