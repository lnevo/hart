The track plans of all three levels, read from the layout plan (version 5.3b) and drawn to scale in centimetres. Where Switch 7 and Switch 13 sit

Neither switch is in the plan. CSX 2121 ran Switch 5 to Switch 15 twice at different speeds, and the occupancy sensors timed each section. With the loco at constant speed between Switch 5 and Switch 15, each switch centre falls where its sensor lit, between the two. The two passes agree within 3 cm.

On the drawing, Switch 7 is at x 134 cm on the straight where the run lies 6.4 cm beside the loop (orange dot), and Switch 13 on the curve into the yard ladder at x 31, y 475 cm. The ring is the earlier Switch 7b estimate from the loop timings, 35 cm further east on the same straight.

Where the plan and the built layout differ

  1. No crossover near Switch 1. The plan has a crossover (T41/T43) between Main West and Track S-R, about 27 in from Switch 1. It was not built, so both tracks are drawn plain: Main West runs from Switch 1 to Switch 23.
  2. The Switch 7 crossover is not in the plan. It joins the two long parallel runs out of Switch 5.
  3. Switch 13 and the engine terminal are not in the plan (Switches 9 and 11, Barn, Scale, EH-1 to EH-3). They sit on the run from Switch 5 to the yard ladder. Switch 13 is marked; the terminal tracks are not drawn, because the plan has no room for them beside the loop.
  4. The two long dead ends past Switches 37 and 39 are the helix lines. On the layout they are the McKeesport and McKees Rocks stubs.

Main level

trackhelix and ramps up to the upper level and down to staging

22 turnouts and 35.3 m of flex at the main level (T300 moved up), with the helix and its ramps in dotted orange (22.3 m of flex). Turnouts show their plan part number; the main level’s own switch numbers and names are not assigned yet.

  • T251 runs on into the inner helix track (McKeesport). The plan has T300 in that track at height 0; it is built on the upper level, so the main level has no T300.
  • The two helix tracks start at T175 (McKees Rocks) and T251 (McKeesport).

Upper level

track

28 turnouts and 50.7 m of flex at 4.25 in (10.8 cm), including the peninsula loop with its yard tracks, which the plan holds at 1 in and is built at 4.25 in. Turnouts show their plan part number; switch numbers and names for this level are not assigned yet.

  • Crossover 23 is the plan’s double crossover at the east end of the peninsula. Its two tracks on the north side run round the east end together: one to T237 and the west, the other to T300, which joins it to the first (T300 is the turnout moved up from the main level; its plan run from the crossover is 292 cm).
