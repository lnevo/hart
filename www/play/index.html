Extra East: Neville Island

From 1899 to 1994 the Pittsburgh & Ohio Valley switched the east end of Neville Island: ore to the car dumper, coke to the furnace, hot metal in ladles, pig iron for the barge. In this edition of Extra East you work one shift on one of its jobs, or sit at the yardmaster's desk at South Yard. Each decision uses a GCOR rule from the C&E study deck or something P&OV crews did, and the source is shown after every answer. The order of each shift is invented.

Daylight · Monday to Friday · 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. · 1981

#105, the ore job

The job the senior men held, on the clean end of the plant. Shove ten-car drags of ore off the South Runner to the Shenango car dumper with SW9 #4, and fuel at South Yard between trips.

Daylight · Monday to Friday · January 1984

#110, the furnace job

The east end, a graphite and coke dust area. Furnace coke over the scale to F1, ladles switched into the furnace's order, hot metal out of the ladle house two at a time, and pig iron on E1 for the barge.

4:00 p.m. to midnight · off Wednesday and Thursday · 1987

#205, second trick

East end and west end as the plant needs. The blog lists no moves for this trick, so the game makes its shift from moves the other jobs made: the PC&Y interchange, the weigh, tank cars on S9 and empty ladles.

Midnight to 7:00 a.m. · off Monday and Tuesday · winter, early 1980s

#305, the midnight job

East and west through the night. A caption names one task, the last: the engine on S9 for sand. In the game it is ten below, a switch is frozen, and the night's coke goes to F1.

The shift changes with the day · August 1979

#115, the relief job

It covers the other jobs' days off. On Saturday it is the daylight crew, and in August 1979 it had SW9 #6. The game gives it an ore drag, tank cars and the scale.

As called · 1988

EXTRA

Called off the extra board when the plant is super busy or the scale is down. Water #4 by hand at the scale house, then whatever the trainmaster needs done.

The yard office · December 1990

Yardmaster at South Yard

A desk the P&OV did not have: its trainmaster wrote the orders and weighed the cars. In the game you work it from the yard office by the scale: orders for #105 and #110, the weigh, scrap gons for A furnace as it comes down, and the PC&Y. B furnace is in its last days.

How a shift works
  • Each shift is a list of work from the switching orders, shown on the right with the plants on the island that year. The map shows where your engine is.
  • A wrong call costs time, a mark, or both. Five marks and the trainmaster takes you off the job. Some mistakes end the shift at once.
  • At the end the shift is scored on time and marks, and your misses are listed with links to the study deck or to the P&OV page.
  • A practice shift is a new draw every time. Today's shift is the same for everyone today; your first try counts, and you can copy the result.
  • The jobs, their hours, the engines, tracks and moves come from Mark Connolly's P&OV blog, collected on the Pittsburgh & Ohio Valley page. The order of each shift, the draws and the events are invented for the game. The P&OV's own rule book is not known, so the rules are GCOR's.
