How the dispatcher panels grew

Two CTC panels, both built during August 2026, shown a step at a time.

HART has two dispatcher panels over the same railroad (see How the signals work). Neither was drawn in one go. Each grew from a few track pieces and station names into the full plant, and the screenshots taken along the way make a short film. Both loop, with no sound.

The JMRI lever machine

This is the stock JMRI CTC machine, a Union Switch & Signal style panel of levers and code buttons over a gold track board. It was built by scripts rather than by hand: Python code running inside JMRI creates the fifteen control points and their locking, and a separate generator draws the track diagram. The pilot was only two locations, Brick and Plane, three columns wide. The rest followed over a single long session, from the evening of 19 August into the early hours of 20 August, and the finished panel carries the railroad's name across the top.

The CATS Digicon panel

The CATS panel is the mouse-driven one. It was drawn in CATS Designer, not generated, so the pictures show a diagram being redrawn: station names first, then the track grows, then the route labels. The set below starts on the night of 8 August 2026 and runs to the titled Rev A drawing of 26 August. Only the cleaner screenshots were used. The panel's later look, with colour and lined routes, is on the signals page.
