HART main level · cut plan

Main level plywood

Every piece of plywood for the main level, laid out on 4 × 8 sheets. Sizes come from the AnyRail plan: the benchwork outlines, the helix bulb, and the two outer main-line tracks for the +4.25 ledge. Each layout was checked in code so every piece fits its sheet with a 1/8 in saw kerf and nothing overlaps.

3/4 in · 0 in level deck 4 sheets

94.3 sq ft of pieces. Includes the balloon-loop subroadbed.

1/2 in · +4.25 level 5 sheets

111.1 sq ft of pieces. The inner main's ramp strip is ripped from the ledge boards (dashed lines); the rest of the offcuts are spare.

Where the pieces go

Please check

  1. The 6 in board (B1, N2) follows the middle of AnyRail's "Helix Top Turn 1" tracks out of the helix, along the neck and through the south opening, and back through the north opening into the north wall deck. It meets the closet deck at the alcove face of each opening. B1 starts square, 1-1/2 in outside the helix edge. Fit that end to the helix exit roadbed. B1 rests on the helix where it meets the post and on a 1x3 joist on the wall stub at the south opening. Once it turns out of the helix it widens to 10 in along the straight, up to the wall face, and goes back to 6 in through the opening. The extra width takes a pair of 3/8 rods 4 in either side of the centre line, clear of both roadbeds, anywhere along the straight. Put a temporary post under it until the rods go in.
  2. Closet deck (B2, B3) is now full over the closet boxes: both alcoves and the turnback, split at the middle of the aisle end so the halves mirror each other. The aisle curve is AnyRail's exact outline.
  3. Balloon loop (F3, G1 to G4) is 9-1/4 in subroadbed, all four pieces the same width, on the AnyRail branchline loop: 28.6 in radius off the west wall, 31 in round the tip, 29 in back to the wall. F3 is a square-cornered block in the SW inside corner (with the 18 in fillet on the aisle side) that carries the loop's first curve, so G1 is a plain rectangle. G4 is a plain rectangle wide enough for the last curve, and G1 to G3 match its width. Their outer edge stays on the 4 in roadbed edge; the extra width is on the inside of the loop. G2 and G3 are pure quarter circles round the tip, nested on sheet D the way the helix roadbed was: chord across the 48 in width, both facing the same way.
  4. The ramp. The outer main stays at +4.25 the whole way. The inner main leaves the N2 crossover at 0, x 125.7, and climbs 2.25% along the north wall, round the NW corner and down the west wall to +4.25 at y 139, ahead of the crossover on L1/L9 (15.8 ft of ramp). The ledge boards are cut full width as before; the dashed line is where to rip each one, 1-1/4 in past the outer main's centre line: the wall side carries the outer main at +4.25, the aisle side is the inner main's ramp strip on risers off the 0 in deck (table below). East of x 125.7 (the east end of L3, and L4) the inner main is at 0 under the ledge, so that aisle strip is scrap.
  5. Trim beside the branchline (dotted line). On the north wall and round the NW corner the 0 in branchline, about 7-3/4 in off the wall, runs under the aisle edge of a full-width ramp strip while the strip is still low. Trim the strip to the dotted line, 1-1/4 in past the inner main's centre line; that leaves about 3/8 in to a 10 ft 8 in wide car on the branchline. On the west wall the strip is high enough (about 3-1/2 in up at y 128 to 139) for the branchline to pass under it: keep those risers on the wall side. Check both with the widest car before gluing.
  6. Helix top (H1, H2, L7) uses AnyRail's exact peninsula outline, including the curved south edge. H1 is the west half of the bulb, 48 × 72. The south-alcove ledge's top edge carries straight west to H1, so L7 is one 83 in piece: the south side of the bulb and neck plus the ledge through the south opening. H2 is the rest, the north-east bulb and the neck's north side out to the wall stubs. Add any access hatch before cutting.
  7. SW top (S1 to S4) covers the peninsula and runs west over W3 to the wall. It's split along the backdrop (AnyRail's view block, 1-1/2 in wide, from the wall to 68-3/4 in out). S2 and S4 are 96 in long, from the tip back toward the wall; the wall strip S1 is one 11-5/8 × 66 piece that the backdrop stands on. The SW corner ledge is one piece (L9) with a straight edge on the SW top, so L9, S1, S2 and S4 meet on straight lines. Put risers under every seam.
  8. Longer pieces. W3 runs on through the closet strip as one 96 in piece (W1 grew to 39-1/2 in to make that work), N2 carries the board through the north opening, W1 includes the NW fillet, and the turnback ledge L5 is one 30 × 84 piece. One joint stays because removing it costs a sheet: the balloon loop's joint at the tip (one half circle needs a fifth 3/4 in sheet).

3/4 in sheets

Each sheet is laid out for the saw: the big piece sits against the factory edges, straight strips are full-length rips, and small pieces sit centred in the big piece's curved offcut with room for the saw. Make the rips first, then the curves. The deck stops 1/8 in off the walls, like the joists, so two 23-7/8 in rips plus the kerf come out of one 48 in sheet. Every butt joint lands on a joist.

Which stock: sheets A and B are the 2 furniture birch sheets on hand, C and D the 2 Baltic birch. The Baltic has no core voids, so it takes the pieces where a void would show or matter: the 6 in board B1 (rods, a span to the wall stub), the closet deck B2 and B3 over the boxes, and the narrow, curved balloon loop subroadbed G1 to G4. The furniture birch takes the wide wall shelves N1, N2, W1 to W3 and F3, which sit on joists 16 in o.c. The two are within about 1/64 in of each other; where they meet (W1 to N1 and W2, N2 to B3 at the north opening, F3 to G1, W3 to G4), sand the high side flush before the cork goes down.

Inner main ramp: risers

Every 12 in along the inner main from x 125.7. Height is the top of the 1/2 in strip above the 0 in deck; the riser goes under the strip, on the deck, and is the height less 0.47 (12 mm ply). The first 21 in has no riser: taper the east end of the strip ripped from L3 to a feather edge so it lies on the deck. The strip's joints are the ledge joints, L3/L2 at x 39 and L2/L1 at y 81.2: each lands on a riser.
