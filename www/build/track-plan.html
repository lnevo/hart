Track plans of the staging, main and upper levels, drawn to scale in centimetres from layout plan version 5.3c. Staging lies 15 in (38.1 cm) below the main level and the upper level 4.25 in (10.8 cm) above it. Two helix tracks join staging to the main level, and a ramp of 4.8 m of flex climbs from the main level to the upper level. The drawings show track only and are generated from the plan file by script; each turnout carries its switch number on the railroad.

01Staging level

trackhelix, below the -5 cm cut

23 switch parts and 72.7 m of flex, with 33.9 m of helix below the -5 cm cut drawn dotted. The plan includes the Switch 7 crossover, Switches 9, 11 and 13, and the engine terminal tracks EH-1 to EH-3. Switches 7, 23 and 35 each have two turnouts that carry the one number.

Plan lengths and timed lengths

Staging is drawn and signalled on the plan’s lengths. JMRI holds a timed length for the blocks a loco was timed over and the plan length for the rest. The test loop was timed over 33 laps (16 for East Ext) with each block taken as its share of the loop, fitted to the turnout positions of plan 5.3b. CSX 2121 timed the Switch 5 to Switch 15 run on two passes at different speeds (steps 24 and 48). The loop blocks add up to 1,382.2 in timed against 1,374.5 in on plan 5.3c.

04Helix

The two helix tracks run from Switches 37 and 39 on staging up to Switches 131A and 131B on the main level, a rise of 15 in (38.1 cm). The steepest piece of flex in any ramp is 2.25%. The run is the plan’s length from switch to switch, flex and straight track together; the schematic draws staging’s share, up to the -5 cm cut, and the main level’s share above it.
