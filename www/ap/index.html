How MMR works

  • DoneBe a current NMRA member (Sunrise Trails Division, Northeastern Region). NMRA rule
  • To doEarn 7 of the 12 certificates, with at least one from each of the four groups below. NMRA rule
  • To doFor each certificate, send a Statement of Qualification (SOQ) with evidence to the Region AP Manager, by way of the Division. NMRA rule Models and scenes need a Merit Award (87.5 of 125 points) from a contest or an AP evaluation.

What to do first

  1. Model Railroad Engineer – Electrical. The closest. Everything is built, wired and drawn, and the SOQ is ready. What is left is a witness.
  2. Chief Dispatcher. 108 operating hours are already logged, with enough conductor, engineer and yard crew time. It needs 10 hours as dispatcher, a freight timetable and a train chart.
  3. Model Railroad Author. The website articles can already give the 21 points allowed from informal work. The STS article in the Division newsletter is the first formal point; the other 20 come from more articles and clinics.
  4. Model Railroad Engineer – Civil. Plan it into the main level, which restarts after Thanksgiving: hand-lay three pieces of trackwork there and ballast 50 ft of finished track.
  5. Master Builder – Cars and Prototype Models. One certificate each from the equipment and setting groups. Neville Island prototype research is already written up for Prototype Models.

Certificate checklists

Model Railroad Engineer – Electrical

Group C10 of 11 done

Build and document the wiring of a layout that runs two trains at once. No merit judging; a witness signs off.

  • DoneRun two mainline trains independently, in both directions. NMRA rule DCC on staging, with the two-track helix and loops: track plan, power districts.
  • DoneFive independent blocks, or for command control enough gaps and switches to keep polarity and allow troubleshooting. NMRA rule 43 detected sections, each gapped in both rails at both ends (drawing D2): tables file, block detectors.
  • DoneA passing siding. NMRA rule Scale, beside the main from Switch 5 to Switch 7 (144 in, about 19 cars); West Ext beside East Lead gives a second passing place: plan P3, track plan.
  • DoneA reversing loop, wye, turntable or transfer table. NMRA rule Two reversing sections on auto-reversers: Main West, and K-1 and K-2: plan P2, power districts, track plan.
  • DoneA yard of three or more tracks with its own lead. NMRA rule South Yard, S-R to S-4: station map.
  • DoneStorage for two locomotives. NMRA rule Engine house EH-1 to EH-3: station map.
  • DoneA protected power supply. NMRA rule Six protected districts on staging and the helix: four circuit breakers, and the NCE AR10 and PSXX-AR on the two reversing sections, which act as their breakers: layout documentation.
  • DoneWire three trackwork items (such as a crossover, turnout frogs, a reversing section). NMRA rule Turnout: every frog is powered through one of the two SPDT contacts on its MTB MP4 machine (D3). Crossover: the a/b crossovers at Switches 23 and 35 in staging, and Switch 7. Other: the two reversing sections on auto-reversers, Main West, and K-1 and K-2. Track plan, power districts.
  • DoneThree of the 23 control items (a to w). NMRA rule (a) Turnout position indication on at least four turnouts: the Touch Toggle panel buttons show each switch machine's position through the MP4's second SPDT contact and a Universal Translator, and the LCOS input boards report it to the CTC desks (LCOS nodes, panels). (b) Occupancy indication on at least five blocks: current-transformer detectors on 43 detected sections, shown on the CATS desks and, until it was parked in the helix-join release (November 2026), the USS CTC desk (block detectors). (v) A computer-to-railroad interface developed for HART: the LCOS-to-MQTT bridge. These three are claimed. Built but kept in reserve in case one is not accepted: (r) the CATS and USS CTC desks (the USS CTC desk is parked since the helix-join release, November 2026) (signals, JMRI additions), (k) computer control, (t) computer-generated block detection and (u) stored control programs.
  • DoneSchematics of the track power circuit and of the six chosen items. NMRA rule Plans P0 to P5 (the staging level to scale and with generic labels) and drawings D1 to D6 (propulsion one-line, typical detected section, turnout frog and indication, crossover with its position indication, reversing section, computer-to-railroad interface) plus the wiring deck pages, in the SOQ package. Kept apart from the railroad's own schematics.
  • ReadySigned witness certification. NMRA rule

Next: the SOQ package was submitted on 15 November 2026 and is ready for the witness visit and the evaluator's signature.

Chief Dispatcher

Group C6 of 10 done

Operate on layouts for 50 hours in several jobs, and prepare the paperwork for an operating layout.

  • Done50 operating hours in all. NMRA rule 108 hours over 34 sessions on other layouts, 2025 and 2026: operations log.
  • DoneAt least 10 hours in each of two jobs besides dispatcher. NMRA rule 81 as conductor and engineer, 18 as yard crew, 9 as yardmaster (one hour short of 10): hours by job.
  • To doAt least 10 hours as dispatcher. NMRA rule None logged yet. Dispatch at HART sessions, or ask for the dispatcher's chair on visits, and add those hours to the log.
  • DoneA layout that runs two mainline trains and yard switching at the same time. NMRA rule At least five HART sessions with dispatcher, yardmaster and local jobs: Operations, session night photos.
  • DoneA schematic with distances. NMRA rule Track plan with block lengths, and the station maps.
  • To doA timetable of at least 8 hours, with at least three scheduled mainline trains each way. NMRA rule TT-23 covers the streetcar only.
  • To doA train chart (time and distance graph) showing at least one meet. NMRA rule
  • DoneA system for moving cars. NMRA rule Car cards and waybills from STS (article).
  • DoneCrew paperwork. NMRA rule Crew sheets, yardmaster sequence and DS-01.
  • To doWitnessed Certificate of Operation. NMRA rule

Next: log 10 hours of dispatching, and write a freight timetable for D749, NVL and CK1 and draw its train chart.
