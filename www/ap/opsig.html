OPSIG Achievement Program

The Operations Special Interest Group runs its own awards for operators, started in 2025. Each Achievement stands alone, with its own certificate. Earning eight, including Conductor & Engineer, makes you a Ninja (Qualified Operator). This page shows where HART stands on each one. For the NMRA certificates, see the NMRA Achievement Program.

How it works

  • DoneBe an OPSIG member. OPSIG rule
  • To doSend one application form per Achievement, with evidence, to OPSIG-AP. Past experience counts. OPSIG rule Some forms need someone else to confirm them.
  • PartlyNinja: eight Achievements, one of them Conductor & Engineer with its exam. OPSIG rule None applied for yet. Yardmaster is met, Host needs one more session, and C&E needs only the exam.

What to do first

  1. Conductor & Engineer. Required for Ninja. The sessions are already logged; register for the exam.
  2. Yardmaster. Met. Apply for it now.
  3. Host. Four sessions are done; the next HART session with two or more invited operators completes it.
  4. Boomer. Ops Around the Clock in Rochester is one operating weekend. Two more are needed.
  5. Traffic Manager and Author. HART's STS car forwarding, and the STS article in The Cannonball, are halfway to each.

Achievement checklists

Host

4 of 5 sessions
  • PartlyPrincipal organiser of five operating sessions, each with at least two invited guest operators. OPSIG rule HART sessions 2 to 5 (1 June, 13 July, 15 August and 8 November 2026), with 3, 5, 5 and 5 attendees; session 1 only seeded the cars into a new STS database. One more is needed: hosted sessions, Operations, session paperwork, session night photos.
  • To doSend the application with session dates. OPSIG rule

Traffic Manager

1 of 2 layouts
  • DoneDesign the traffic system for one layout. OPSIG rule HART's shipper orders, waybills and car cards from STS (article).
  • To doDesign one for a second layout. OPSIG rule The Operations Pro railroad built as a test is not a real layout; a friend's layout would be.

Author

1 of 2 articles
  • PartlyTwo original works with a meaningful focus on operations: articles published by someone else, blog posts of 1,000 words or more, or videos of ten minutes or more. OPSIG rule The STS car forwarding article was published in The Cannonball, the Sunrise Trails Division newsletter. A second operations article is needed, for example in The Dispatcher's Office; the car cards article is a candidate.

Conductor & Engineer

sessions done, exam left

Required for Ninja.

  • DoneTen sessions as conductor or engineer, on three different layouts. Your own layout and home club do not count. OPSIG rule A&W is the home club, so its sessions do not count. Away from it: 14 conductor and engineer sessions in 2025 and 2026 (Ops Around the Clock is one entry covering three layouts) plus 3 as yard crew, on twelve layouts: operations log.
  • To doPass the exam: timetable and train order, DTC, track warrants, CTC and general rules. OPSIG rule Register by email with OPSIG-AP; study material is linked from their C&E page. Practice with the C&E exam study deck: flashcards and practice tests drawn from the seven study guides.

Mentor

not logged
  • ConfirmBe the dedicated helper to a new or less experienced operator for a whole session, on three occasions. A written statement from the host or the mentee goes with the application. OPSIG rule Possible at HART: pairing a newcomer with the owner, using the Primer. Not yet checked whether a host’s own sessions count.

Dispatcher

0 of 3 layouts
  • To doDispatch on three layouts (your own and your home club count), showing three systems such as DTC, CTC, timetable and train order, or warrants. The host verifies it. OPSIG rule None in the operations log yet. HART (CTC) may count as one. The other two need different systems, such as timetable and train order or track warrants.

Yardmaster

3 of 3 layouts
  • DoneWork as yardmaster on three different layouts. OPSIG rule Three yardmaster sessions, each on a different layout (a home club counts here), in 2025 and 2026: operations log.
  • To doSend the application with the sessions. OPSIG rule

Office Crew

not logged
  • ConfirmWork an office job (clerk, agent, operator) at three different sessions. Dispatcher and yardmaster do not count. OPSIG rule

Boomer

1 of 3 weekends
  • PartlyAttend three formal operating weekends or multi-session events outside your home area, usually more than 100 miles from home. OPSIG rule Ops Around the Clock in Rochester (2025, three layouts): operations log. Earlier or later years there count as more weekends.

Clinician

0 of 3 clinics
  • To doGive three clinics to a live audience, in person or online, on topics relevant to operations. OPSIG rule Ready-made subjects: STS car forwarding, the virtual track scale, the CATS CTC desk. A clinic also counts toward the NMRA Author certificate.

Volunteer

not logged
  • ConfirmHelp with or organise three operations events. OPSIG rule
