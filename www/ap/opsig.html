OPSIG Achievement Program

The Operations Special Interest Group runs its own awards for operators, started in 2025. Each Achievement stands alone, with its own certificate. Earning eight, including Conductor & Engineer, makes you a Ninja (Qualified Operator). This page shows where HART stands on each one. For the NMRA certificates, see the NMRA Achievement Program.

How it works

  • ConfirmBe an OPSIG member.
  • To doSend one application form per Achievement, with evidence, to OPSIG-AP. Past experience counts. Some forms need someone else to confirm them, which can take 30 days or more.
  • PartlyNinja: eight Achievements, one of them Conductor & Engineer with its exam. None applied for yet. Host and Yardmaster are met, and C&E needs only the exam.

What to do first

  1. Conductor & Engineer. Required for Ninja. The sessions are already logged; register for the exam.
  2. Host and Yardmaster. Both are met. Apply for them now.
  3. Boomer. Ops Around the Clock in Rochester is one operating weekend. Two more are needed.
  4. Traffic Manager and Author. HART's STS car forwarding and its articles are halfway to each.

Achievement checklists

Host

5 of 5 sessions
  • DonePrincipal organiser of five operating sessions. HART sessions 1 to 5: Operations, session paperwork, session night photos.
  • To doSend the application with session dates.

Traffic Manager

1 of 2 layouts
  • DoneDesign the traffic system for one layout. HART's shipper orders, waybills and car cards from STS (article).
  • To doDesign one for a second layout. The Operations Pro railroad built as a test is not a real layout; a friend's layout would be.

Author

0 of 2 articles
  • PartlyTwo widely circulated articles that are mostly about operations. The STS and car cards articles are on this site. Ask OPSIG whether that counts as widely circulated, or place them in The Dispatcher's Office or a Division newsletter.

Conductor & Engineer

sessions done, exam left

Required for Ninja.

  • DoneTen sessions as conductor or engineer, on three different layouts. Your own layout and home club do not count. 18 conductor and engineer sessions in 2025 and 2026 on eleven layouts (Ops Around the Clock is one entry covering three), plus four as yard conductor or engineer. Ten of them are away from A&W, if that counts as a home club: operations log.
  • To doPass the exam: timetable and train order, DTC, track warrants, CTC and general rules. Register by email with OPSIG-AP; study material is linked from their C&E page.

Mentor

not logged
  • ConfirmMentor a new operator one to one in three sessions. Pairing a newcomer with you at HART, using the Primer, would do it.

Dispatcher

0 of 3 layouts
  • To doDispatch on three layouts using three different systems. None in the operations log yet. HART (CTC) may count as one. The other two need different systems, such as timetable and train order or track warrants.

Yardmaster

3 of 3 layouts
  • DoneWork as yardmaster on three different layouts. Three yardmaster sessions, each on a different layout, in 2025 and 2026: operations log.
  • To doSend the application with the sessions.

Office Crew

not logged
  • ConfirmWork an office job (car clerk, agent, crew caller) at three different sessions.

Boomer

1 of 3 weekends
  • PartlyAttend three operating weekends, usually more than 100 miles from home. Ops Around the Clock in Rochester (2025, three layouts): operations log. Earlier or later years there count as more weekends.

Clinician

0 of 3 clinics
  • To doGive three clinics mostly about operations. Ready-made subjects: STS car forwarding, the virtual track scale, the CATS CTC desk. A clinic also counts toward the NMRA Author certificate.

Volunteer

not logged
  • ConfirmHelp with or organise three operations events.

Key Helper

—
  • ConfirmGiven by a layout owner who is an OPSIG member, usually to up to three helpers per layout. You can nominate HART's own regular helpers.
